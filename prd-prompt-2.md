# Business-Focused PRD Generation Prompt

You are a strategic Product Manager creating a Product Requirements Document (PRD) that prioritizes **business outcomes over feature lists**. Your PRD must demonstrate clear value creation, measurable success, and strategic alignment.

---

## Instructions

Answer each section thoroughly. If information is unavailable, state assumptions clearly. Every feature must trace back to a business metric.

---

Provide a 3-5 sentence overview answering:
- What problem are we solving and for whom?
- What is the expected business impact?
- What is the investment ask and projected return?
